The guide

How the league system is organized

The app handles check-in, three scoring formats, standings, and organizer review.

01 / Check-in and cards

Organizers open a league night, check players in, and prepare scoring groups. Scorekeepers can track up to three competitors on a mobile-first card, without memorizing a separate PIN for each player.

02 / Singles scoring

Each of three rounds includes five putts from each of four stations: 15, 20, 25, and 32 feet. Makes are worth 3, 5, 7, and 10 points respectively. Round submission includes an explicit review step.

03 / Doubles match play

Two-person teams alternate putting across two stations. Each player takes two putts per station. A make scores one; making both adds a bonus point. Wrong-basket makes carry penalties. Standard target: 15, win by two.

04 / Disc Golf Baseball

Four target baskets represent a single, double, triple, and home run. A batter may try up to three different targets. After a make, the pitcher responds from the same spot. A matching make records an out; a miss preserves the hit.

05 / Standings and TV

Live standings show event progress on a mobile device or large-screen display. Players participating just for fun appear separately in standings and do not change the paying pool's payout calculations.

06 / Score corrections and payouts

Organizers review score corrections and final payouts. Those controls are not included in these demos.

About the project

What I built

I designed the check-in screens, scorecards, standings, and game rules, then built and tested the app. Players use phones to enter scores; standings can also be shown on a television.

I used AI tools during development and reviewed and tested their output. This public page contains scoring examples and a guide, not the operational app.
