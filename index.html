Learning to use AI with judgment.

I created Applied AI Lab to teach people how AI systems work, how to check their outputs, and when human review is necessary. Foundations introduces these ideas; Studio asks learners to apply them to workplace problems.

How I lead the work

I created and own Applied AI Lab. I set its purpose, scope, and learning standards, then directed the work from course design through development, testing, and publication. I used AI tools to assist development and reviewed the resulting content and behavior.

My work has included deciding what to build first, establishing criteria for acceptable performance, and revising the product after learner testing. I required open access to every scenario, optional reporting, and recovery of saved work. I also set an editorial standard: explain the ideas clearly without removing the technical distinctions learners need to understand.

I approach AI education as a program that needs direction, evidence, and continued review. A course must teach the right material, give people useful practice, and provide a way to judge what they can do independently. Those decisions draw on my experience teaching, leading programs, and evaluating AI work.
