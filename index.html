About the project

Applied AI Lab brings together instructional design and practical AI program management. Its exercises emphasize accurate technical reasoning, clear communication across audiences, and the judgment needed to move from a proposed use case to a responsible implementation.

The learning experiences use progressive instructional support without locking learners into a fixed sequence. The project is an independent educational portfolio artifact, not an official training product of any employer.
