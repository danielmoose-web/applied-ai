My role in the project

I am Daniel Moose, the creator and project owner of Applied AI Lab. I defined the learning goals, designed the instructional progression and scenarios, directed the visual design, and tested and refined the learner experience. I used AI tools to support development, with responsibility for the project’s direction and review remaining with me.

For recruiters and hiring teams, this project provides a working example of how I connect teaching experience with AI education and implementation: explaining technical ideas clearly, designing meaningful practice, incorporating human oversight, and improving a product through testing and feedback.

Foundations introduces responsible AI use. Studio extends that work into technical design and program decisions through eight independently accessible scenarios. Instructional support decreases as learners take on more responsibility, while reference resources remain available.

This is my independent educational portfolio project. It is not an official training product of any employer.
